# Neelagiri — AI Recipe Platform 🍽️✨

Neelagiri is a full‑stack recipe platform that combines curated discovery, AI-assisted cooking features, pantry intelligence, and a clean modern UI. It’s designed for people who want quick inspiration, better meal planning, and a smooth “from idea → ingredients → cooking” experience.

**Frontend:** Next.js (App Router) • **Backend:** Strapi v5 • **Database:** PostgreSQL

---

# 🧱 Tech Stack
- **Web App:** Next.js + React
- **UI:** Tailwind CSS + shadcn/ui (Radix UI)
- **AI:** Gemini (Google Generative AI SDK)
- **Backend/CMS:** Strapi v5
- **Database:** PostgreSQL

---

# 🌟 Project Highlights

## 🍲 Recipe Discovery & Cooking
- Structured recipe pages designed for fast reading while cooking
- Clear recipe presentation with category/cuisine context when available
- Smooth “Start Cooking” experience focused on usability

## 🔥 Today’s Special (Recipe of the Day)
- A dedicated “Today’s Special” page that showcases a curated recipe
- Global inspiration: recipes selected from different cuisines/regions
- Built for quick decisions when you don’t know what to cook today

## 🤖 AI Powered by Gemini
- AI recipe generation when a recipe isn’t already available
- AI pantry scanning (vision) to detect ingredients from images
- AI recipe ideas based on the ingredients you already have

## 🧺 Smart Pantry
- Add ingredients and keep them organized in one place
- Edit/update quantities and remove items anytime
- Filter/search your pantry list for quick access
- Generate cooking ideas directly from pantry ingredients

## 💎 Subscriptions (Free & Pro)
- Free and Pro tiers supported
- Pro tier unlocks higher limits (including unlimited AI pantry scans where enabled)
- Pro‑locked sections to protect premium features and guide upgrades
- A dedicated Subscriptions/Plans area for upgrades and plan visibility

## 📄 Recipe PDF Export
- Export recipes to a clean PDF format for sharing, printing, or offline use

## 📱 Mobile Responsive
- Responsive layouts optimized for mobile, tablet, and desktop
